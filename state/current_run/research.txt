The tiny ball at the tip needs a small amount of give so it can indent the paper, enlarge the contact patch, roll smoothly, and pull viscous oil‑based ink from the reservoir onto the page. A single sheet of paper or a hard surface (desk, glass) is too stiff; the ball slips, the contact area stays tiny, and ink transfer stops or becomes very faint. Adding one or more sheets (or any compliant backing) lets the paper flex, creates a deeper indentation, and restores reliable ink flow.
