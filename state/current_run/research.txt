MECHANISM / CAUSE AND EFFECT":"PVS results from a bias in the perceptual decision process. Signal‑detection theory predicts that to avoid missing a real vibration the brain lowers its criterion, increasing false alarms. Predictive coding adds that the somatosensory cortex receives a strong top‑down prediction of vibration; when the actual tactile signal is weak or absent, the prediction dominates, producing the phantom sensation. Stress and dopamine further lower the criterion and amplify the prediction, especially in high‑alert environments.","4.
