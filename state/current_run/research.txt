{"1. CORE ANSWER":"The sensation of a phone vibrating when it hasn’t is a perceptual illusion known as phantom vibration syndrome (PVS). It arises when the brain’s predictive coding system, primed by frequent phone use and heightened attention to notifications, interprets normal tactile or unrelated stimuli as a vibration. PVS is common, benign, and linked to contextual factors (e.g., waiting for a call, noisy environments) and usage patterns (e.g., high frequency, always‑on habits).","2.
